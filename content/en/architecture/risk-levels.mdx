# Risk Levels and Verdicts

The scorer ([`pipeline.rs`](https://github.com/IDjinn/sentry/blob/main/crates/sentry-core/src/pipeline.rs))
sums the weights of the [`Signal`](https://github.com/IDjinn/sentry/blob/main/crates/sentry-core/src/analysis.rs#L77)s
produced by rules, heuristics, trackers and forks into a `risk_score`
(0–100) and maps it to a
[`RiskLevel`](https://github.com/IDjinn/sentry/blob/main/crates/sentry-core/src/analysis.rs#L18).
The [`VerdictPolicy`](https://github.com/IDjinn/sentry/blob/main/crates/sentry-core/src/policy.rs)
turns the level into the final verdict, which can then be overridden by
policy and by repeat-offender escalation.

## Score → level → verdict

| Score | Level | Color (TUI) | Default verdict (`[policy]`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–9 | Info | gray | `allow` |
| 10–29 | Low | blue | `allow` |
| 30–49 | Medium | yellow | `rate_limit` |
| 50–74 | High | orange | `challenge` |
| 75–100 | Critical | red | `block` |

- The level buckets are fixed (stable dashboards); the **verdict per
  level** is configurable in `[policy]` (`info`/`low`/`medium`/`high`/
  `critical`), and ordered `[[policy.override]]` entries (DSL `match` →
  `verdict`) win on first match.
- Short-circuit rules enter with a fixed score: Block = 100,
  Challenge = 50, RateLimit = 30, Quarantine = 40
  ([`process`](https://github.com/IDjinn/sentry/blob/main/crates/sentry-core/src/pipeline.rs#L465)).
- The sum is **capped at 100** and gains a repetition bonus in a sliding
  window (`repetition_window_secs`, 60 s) when the same IP repeats
  signals.

### The `PostureAdvisory` nuance (F11)

Posture advisories describe the **protected site** (missing security
headers on the upstream response), never the visitor. The weight is the
`POSTURE_ADVISORY_WEIGHT = 0` constant and
[`weight_for`](https://github.com/IDjinn/sentry/blob/main/crates/sentry-core/src/pipeline.rs#L815)
returns 0 **before** consulting `[scorer.weights]` — no configuration can
turn an advisory into enforcement. The signal shows up in dashboards,
metrics and `sentry posture`, but never changes score, verdict or
webhooks.

## Repeat-offender escalation (F2.12)

Every event whose final verdict is not `Allow` records one strike in the
IP's [`OffenderTracker`](https://github.com/IDjinn/sentry/blob/main/crates/sentry-core/src/offender.rs).
Strikes expire after `window_secs` (default **7 days** — outlives the 24 h
edge-rule TTL). Escalation **only raises** the verdict:

- `challenge_at` (default **3**) strikes → at least Challenge.
- `block_at` (default **5**) strikes → Block.
- `persist = true` mirrors strikes to the `ip_state` table (Postgres) and
  pre-warms the tracker on startup — a returning offender past the edge
  TTL is re-blocked on its first violating event.

`Allow` never records a strike (no ratchet against benign traffic).

## Guards that override everything

- **Trusted IPs** (F7.2): IPs in `[real_ip] trusted_ips` get an immediate
  Allow and are immune even to `rescore_from` — the "you can't lock
  yourself out" guard.
- **Shadow IPs**: `Block`/`Quarantine` are downgraded to `Challenge`
  (`cap_shadow`) — fully analyzed and logged, never banned.
- **Blacklist**: an IP on `[real_ip] blacklist` is blocked (score 100)
  before any detector.

## Cross-IP scan→attack correlation (F3.10)

The "shot calling" pattern observed in honeypots: a scan (404 sweep,
masscan/zmap SYNs, …) from a "clean" IP followed by an exploit/brute-force
from **another** IP in the same /24 (IPv4), /64 (IPv6) or ASN within the
window emits `ScanAttackCorrelation` (weight 20) — an aggravator of the
attack, never a standalone detector. SYNs captured by the `tcp` source
feed the same window as HTTP sweeps.

```toml
[correlation]
enabled = true
window_secs = 900           # 15 min
```

Alongside comes the scanner taxonomy as reputation tiers: `authorized`
(a contracted scanner — no signal; trust with `reputation = "authorized"`
→ allow) and `promiscuous` (publishes recon for anyone to query →
`PromiscuousScanner`, weight 10 — a scanner that publishes recon for
anyone is not benign).
